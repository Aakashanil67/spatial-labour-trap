# P1.2: extended horizon and initial-condition assessment

Generated by `scripts/diagnose_horizon.py`. Extends P1.1 (`results/review_v2/temporal_p1_1/`,
600 steps, three `household_inflow` settings) along the two axes P1.1 could not reach: horizon
length, and initial conditions. Three campaigns, all at the fitted parameter vector
(`results/published/calibration_result.json` over `configs/baseline.yaml`), validation seeds
1001-1003:

- `results/review_v2/temporal_p1_2_horizon1200/` -- 1,200 steps, published initial condition.
- `results/review_v2/temporal_p1_2_horizon2400/` -- 2,400 steps, published initial condition.
- `results/review_v2/temporal_p1_2_initcond/` -- 1,200 steps, crossing three
  `initial_search_capital` multipliers (0.5x / 1x / 2x the fitted 1.4962) with two
  initialisation schemes (standard all-searching start, and a same-seed donor run 600 steps then
  transplanted into a fresh recipient -- "mixed state").

**Resource balance holds exactly throughout, as in P1.1.** 1.8M + 3.6M + 10.8M = 16.2M
agent-steps checked across the three campaigns, zero violations, maximum discrepancy 0.0.

## 1. The discouraged-share drift found at 600 steps (Question 1's original A7/P1.1 evidence)
##    plateaus; mean search capital keeps converging, slowly

Extending the published run to 1,200 and then 2,400 steps:

| seed | window 601-750 | window 1051-1200 | window 1801-1950 | window 2251-2400 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1001 | 0.3499 | 0.3283 | 0.3602 | 0.3709 |
| 1002 | 0.3523 | 0.3445 | 0.3330 | 0.3419 |
| 1003 | 0.3563 | 0.3509 | 0.3182 | 0.3335 |

`hard_discouraged_share` moves inside a 0.29-0.37 band from window 601-750 onward with no further
directional trend: regressing each seed's windowed value on window midpoint for windows starting
at 750+ gives slopes of -0.003, +0.006, -0.009 per 1,000 steps (r = -0.06, 0.22, -0.31) --
statistically indistinguishable from noise given a within-band standard deviation of 0.014-0.025.
This is a materially different picture from the steep rise reported for windows 1-600 in the
original Question 1 evidence, and answers the "does it plateau" question the plan set up before
authorising the 2,400-step extension: **yes, by around step 750-900, and the plateau holds for
another 1,500+ steps.**

`mean_search_capital` is not yet fully flat: the same regression (windows 750+) gives slopes of
-0.0021, -0.0044, -0.0009 per 1,000 steps with r = -0.59, -0.81, -0.55 -- small in magnitude
(against a level of 0.016-0.017) but real, not noise. It is converging asymptotically toward a
floor near 0.015-0.018, not still falling at anything like its earlier rate. Section 3 shows this
floor is a structural property of the discouragement-cycle mechanism, not an artefact of a
particular initial condition.

## 2. Why there is a floor: the discouragement cycle is mechanically bounded

`reentry_threshold` (0.035) and `household_inflow` (0.008) are fixed, non-estimated parameters.
A discouraged agent gains exactly `household_inflow` per step and re-enters `SEARCHING` the step
its capital first reaches `reentry_threshold`, so a discouraged spell cannot exceed
`ceil(0.035 / 0.008) = 5` months. Verified both theoretically and empirically against a live run
(500 agents, 600 steps, seed 1001): **16,149 completed discouraged spells, maximum observed
length 5, zero exceptions**, distribution `{4: 5024, 5: 11125}`.

The searching phase that follows is not symmetrically bounded -- its length depends on
`cost_per_trip` (which varies with `distance_to_cbd`, fixed 0.00251 at the CBD up to about
0.00541 at the maximum township distance of 20 grid units under the fitted
`search_cost_per_trip`) and on how many of the up-to-`max_trips_per_step=3` affordable trips an
agent's belief-driven intensity actually spends. A chronically unemployed agent therefore cycles:
a short (mechanism-dependent, not fixed) searching burst funded by the ~0.035-0.043 capital it
re-entered with, followed by a hard-bounded 4-5 step discouraged recovery, repeating for as long
as it is not hired. That recurrent cycle -- not indefinite depletion, and not full recovery -- is
what produces a stable low, non-zero population-mean capital once enough of the population has
cycled through it at least once: exactly the mechanism the original Question 1 evidence flagged as
one of two candidate explanations (the "recurrent structure" hypothesis) and could not, at 600
steps, distinguish from unbounded drift.

## 3. Initial conditions: long-run outcomes are shared, but the published window is not

**Long run (window 1051-1200), same seed, across every initial-capital multiplier and
initialisation scheme:**

| seed | employment_share (all arms) | hard_discouraged_share (all-searching, 0.5x/1x/2x) | hard_discouraged_share (mixed-state, 0.5x/1x/2x) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1001 | 0.4320 (all-searching) / 0.4240 (mixed) | 0.329 / 0.328 / 0.325 | 0.333 / 0.334 / 0.333 |
| 1002 | 0.4031 (all-searching) / 0.4137 (mixed) | 0.345 / 0.345 / 0.337 | 0.339 / 0.339 / 0.338 |
| 1003 | 0.3993 (all-searching) / 0.3952 (mixed) | 0.351 / 0.351 / 0.345 | 0.353 / 0.353 / 0.353 |

`employment_share` is bit-identical across every capital multiplier within an initialisation
scheme (reconfirming P1.1's vacancy-constrained finding under a new axis of variation), and
`hard_discouraged_share` at this window varies by at most 0.008 across the three capital
multipliers for a given seed and initialisation scheme (computed directly from
`window_summary.csv`, not eyeballed) -- against a between-seed range (averaging over multiplier
and scheme) of about 0.021. **By step ~1,100, the long-run discouraged-share outcome does not
depend on where the population started; it depends on the seed, the way any Monte Carlo quantity
should.**

`mean_search_capital` also converges to the same floor eventually, but slowly when the starting
point is far from it. At window 1051-1200, the fresh (all-searching) 2x-capital arm is still at
0.035-0.084 -- two to five times the 0.015-0.018 floor the 0.5x and 1x arms have already reached
-- while its per-window trajectory (2.04 to 1.20 to 0.68 to 0.40 to 0.25 to 0.14 to 0.08 to 0.06,
seed 1001) shows a roughly geometric decay of about 0.6x per 150-step window, implying full convergence only somewhere in
the range of roughly 1,500-1,900 steps on that extrapolation, not by 1,200 -- not verified by a
run that far, so treated as an estimate, not a measured fact. The mixed-state 2x arm, whose donor
already spent 600 steps decaying before transplant, is much closer to the floor by the same
window (0.017-0.030) -- consistent with the same decay process, just started earlier.

**The published measurement window (51-150) tells a completely different story.** Under the
standard all-searching start, `hard_discouraged_share` at this window is:

| seed | 0.5x capital | 1x capital (published) | 2x capital |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1001 | 0.275 | 0.118 | 0.013 |
| 1002 | 0.298 | 0.136 | 0.022 |
| 1003 | 0.292 | 0.136 | 0.025 |

A more than twenty-fold range (0.013 to 0.30), driven by nothing but the assumed initial capital,
at exactly the window the published calibration is fitted against. Under the mixed-state start,
where 600 steps of the same decay process have already run before this window is measured, the
same three multipliers give 0.31-0.36 in every case -- the sensitivity is essentially gone.

## 4. What this settles for Question 1, and what it does not

This is direct, quantitative evidence for a specific mechanism, not a restatement of the original
A7 concern:

- **The model has not reached its long-run regime by step 150.** The long-run discouraged-share
  band (~0.29-0.37) and capital floor (~0.015-0.018) are common across every initial condition
  tested, but the published window sits inside a transient that takes on the order of 600-1,000
  steps to largely dissipate (longer for the capital level specifically, given the 2x arm's slow
  approach).
- **`initial_search_capital`'s identifying power over `discouraged_share` comes specifically from
  measuring inside that transient.** The same parameter change that moves the published-window
  discouraged share by over 20x (0.013 to 0.30) moves the long-run (window 1051-1200) value by at
  most 0.008 for a given seed and initialisation scheme. This is consistent with, and gives a
  mechanistic account for, the parameter's estimate sitting
  at its calibration upper bound (1.4962 against a box of [0.2, 1.5], flagged
  `boundary_adjacent_params` in `calibration_result.json`) -- the estimation is pushing it as far
  as the box allows to suppress early discouragement, which is exactly the lever this evidence
  shows it has, only in the transient.
- **This does not by itself decide stationary-economy versus transition-study.** Both readings
  remain internally consistent with everything shown here. What it does is remove one candidate
  version of the stationary reading: a stationary-economy framing that keeps
  `initial_search_capital` as an estimated parameter disciplining the *published* moments would be
  estimating a parameter whose entire identifying power comes from a transient the framing says
  should not be measured. A transition-study framing, or a stationary framing that measures its
  moments only after the transient has passed (window 1051-1200 or later, not 51-150), would not
  have this problem -- at the cost of measuring a different, and empirically much later, window
  than any survey estimate this thesis's targets are drawn from.

**Still not decided.** This report does not choose between stationary and transition framings, or
resolve what (if anything) about the resource rules should change. It supplies the evidence P1.2
was scoped to produce; the choice itself is Question 1's, made explicitly, not defaulted into by
which run happened to get built first.
